**Bruno Escobar**, founder of ZeroKeyUSB and Depbit LLC

Bruno Escobar created ZeroKeyUSB as his undergraduate thesis in Industrial Design Engineering and Product Development at the University of Cádiz, where it won the TFG/TFM award at the 16th edition of the university's atrÉBT! entrepreneurship awards. He kept developing it for years in the open, sharing early prototypes on Hackaday.io, and redesigned the hardware, enclosure, firmware and browser tools until it was ready for production. Today he runs the project through Depbit LLC, with manufacturing in Spain.
