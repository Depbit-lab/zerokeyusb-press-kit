# ZeroKeyUSB passes 250% of its goal on Crowd Supply: an offline, open-source password vault born at the University of Cádiz

**FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE** · October 2026

ZeroKeyUSB, a physical, cloud-free, open-source password manager, has passed 250% of its funding goal on Crowd Supply.

**What it is.** A USB-C device that shows up as a keyboard and types your credentials for you. It stores up to 61 passwords with AES-128 encryption, protected by a PIN, a 128×32 OLED display and five capacitive touch buttons. No app, account, internet or installation is needed. It works with computers, phones and tablets.

**Why it matters.** Cloud password managers put every credential in one attractive target. ZeroKeyUSB keeps them on a device that never goes online.

**Open by design.** Schematics, firmware and hardware files are released under the MIT license.

**From thesis to product.** It started as an award-winning undergraduate thesis at the University of Cádiz and is now developed by Depbit LLC.
